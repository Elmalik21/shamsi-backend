## The Problem

**90% of Egypt's 15,000 solar installers use Excel and WhatsApp to design systems.**

Professional tools (PVsyst $125/mo, HelioScope $250/mo) are unaffordable, English-only, and contain no Egyptian climate data. Every manual design takes 2–4 hours and produces results 15–20% worse than optimal — costing the industry an estimated $150M/year in suboptimal solar installations.

---

## Our Solution

**Shamsi Smart** delivers professional, bank-ready solar system designs in 5 minutes using three AI models:

- **YOLOv8** — analyses roof photos to detect usable area, obstructions, and shading (94.7% accuracy)
- **CNN-LSTM** — predicts annual energy yield from Egypt's climate data (4.2% MAPE)
- **NSGA-II** — optimises across 2M+ configurations to return 5 Pareto-optimal designs

**Output:** PVsyst files, PDF report, Excel analysis — everything needed for bank financing, in one click.

**Validated:** 3.1% mean error vs PVWatts v5 across 5 Egyptian cities. Paper under review at *Applied Energy*.
