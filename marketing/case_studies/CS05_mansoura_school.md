# Case Study CS-05: Mansoura School — Government Building
## Free Tier Powers Educational Solar — Zero Budget Design

**Location:** Mansoura, Dakahlia Governorate  
**System:** 30 kWp (52 panels)  
**Designer:** School headmaster with no engineering background  
**Design time:** 12 minutes  
**Tier used:** Free (3 optimisations/month)

---

## Background

A government secondary school in Mansoura had received a Ministry of Education grant to install rooftop solar to reduce electricity costs. The school's headmaster, Magdy (not an engineer), was tasked with preparing a technical proposal for the grant application — without a budget for engineering consultants.

Magdy found Shamsi Smart through a Facebook post in an Egyptian educators' group. He had never designed a solar system. He completed the entire proposal in 12 minutes using the free tier.

## Why This Case Matters

This case demonstrates Shamsi Smart's accessibility beyond the professional engineering market:
- **No engineering background required**
- **No cost** (free tier, 3 optimisations/month)
- **Complete, bank-ready output** for grant application
- Represents the "democratisation" of professional solar design tools

## The Design Process

**Roof data:** Magdy measured the school roof himself (60 m × 20 m = 1,200 m²), sketched it on paper, and entered the approximate usable area (800 m² after estimating classrooms, staircases, and a water tank) manually — he didn't have satellite imagery access.

**System requirements:**
- Grid-connected net-metering
- Monthly consumption: ~8,500 kWh (school year) / ~1,200 kWh (summer)
- Budget: Determined by grant, not fixed
- Priority: Minimum payback (grant reviewer criteria)

**Shamsi output (best payback option):**
- 52 panels × 580Wp = 30.16 kWp
- 2 × Huawei SUN2000-17KTL-M2
- Annual yield: 43,100 kWh
- Self-consumption: ~72% (school-year weighted)
- Annual savings: 32,600 EGP (at educational institution tariff, ~0.28 EGP/kWh)
- Total cost: 266,000 EGP
- Simple payback: 8.2 years
- Grant ROI: Positive from Year 1

**Exported:** PDF report for the Ministry grant application. The PDF included the system schematic, monthly yield chart, and financial summary — exactly what the grant template required.

## Grant Application Outcome

The Ministry of Education reviewer noted the professional quality of the proposal and specifically cited the "detailed financial analysis and monthly yield projections" as strengthening the application.

**Grant approved:** Full 266,000 EGP funding.

System installed by a local Mansoura company (who was shown the Shamsi report by the school and asked to build exactly to that spec).
